# Afro-Asian Insurance Services brand assets

The house brand — Afro-Asian Insurance Services Ltd, broker at Lloyd's —
leads the brand lockup on every screen (`frontend/src/BrandLockup.jsx`,
`HOUSE_BRAND` in `frontend/src/theme.js`). Self-hosted here so nothing loads
from outside at runtime.

- `logo.png` — the logo as supplied (764 × 260), its white ground made
  transparent so it sits on the top bar and the login card in every theme;
  dark themes put it on a white plate. Replace it with a higher-resolution
  original or an SVG when one is to hand — keep the name.
- `favicon.png` — the browser-tab icon: the globe cut from `logo.png` at
  64 × 64. Replace it with the firm's own favicon when one is to hand.
